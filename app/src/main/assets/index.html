▶ YouTube & video tools

EMBED + LOCAL

Play an embedded YouTube video

Paste a YouTube video URL. This uses YouTube's official embed, when the creator and YouTube allow it.

YouTube embeds do not let this page reliably remove ads, force quality, control playback speed, or automatically skip sponsor segments. Those capabilities are not claimed here.

Community video information

Try checking community dislike estimates and SponsorBlock segment metadata for a video ID. These public services may be unavailable, rate-limited, or unable to return data. Metadata does not control the official YouTube player.

Local video player

About downloading

You can save files from sources that explicitly permit downloading, and play supported files offline. NextPlay does not download YouTube video/audio streams. Use YouTube's official offline/download features where available or obtain files from authorized sources.
